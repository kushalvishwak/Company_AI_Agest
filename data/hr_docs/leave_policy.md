# Leave Policy

Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per
calendar year, accrued monthly. Leave requests should be filed at least
three working days in advance through the internal assistant or the HR
portal.

Sick leave is tracked separately: employees get 10 days of paid sick
leave per year, and a doctor's note is required for any sick leave
lasting more than two consecutive days.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward into the next calendar year
up to a maximum of 5 days; anything beyond that is forfeited unless
local law requires otherwise.

Managers approve leave for their direct reports. The assistant can file
a leave request on an employee's behalf, but every request is a write
action and requires the employee's explicit confirmation before it is
submitted.
